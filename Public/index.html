⚡ How TurboConvert Achieves Sub-Second MOV to MP4 Conversion

01

Direct Stream Remuxing

Apple MOV and MP4 share the same underlying ISO Base Media file structure and codecs (H.264 / HEVC). Instead of slowly re-compressing millions of pixels, our engine repackages the packets directly at disk read/write speeds (1,000x - 5,000x real-time speed).

02

Zero Lossless Fidelity

Because no re-encoding occurs in stream copy mode, quality loss is mathematically 0.00%. Every frame, keyframe, and color profile remains 100% identical to the original recording.

03

Smart PCM Audio Fallback

Many professional cameras record uncompressed PCM audio into MOV containers. Standard MP4 players require AAC. Our smart engine copies the heavy 4K video instantly while transcoding only the audio stream in ~1 second.
